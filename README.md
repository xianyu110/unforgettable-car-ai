## What it does

- **1 selfie (@image1) + optional car photo (@image2)** → you flash cards **2** and **4**, swipe the lens, and the car appears behind you
- ~**15s** MP4, 9:16 or 16:9, typically 720p; scenes: street / rooftop / car park
- 自拍 + 可选爱车 → 亮出 2 和 4、手扫镜头、车出现在身后
- Failed renders refund credits

1. Open [unforgettablecarai.cc](https://unforgettablecarai.cc)

3. Pick a scene and aspect ratio → Create
4. Checkout with a one-time pack (Stripe)
